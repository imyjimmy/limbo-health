Limbo Health Support

Use this page to ask questions or request support for the Limbo Health app.

Contact

Support email
support@limbo.health

limbohealth.com
Email support@limbo.health if you need help with sign-in, records-request workflows, generated PDFs, account deletion questions, or app issues.

What we can help with

  • Sign-in and account-access problems
  • Medical-records request workflow issues
  • Generated PDF or sharing problems
  • Questions about local storage, encryption keys, or account deletion
  • App crashes or launch failures

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • Your app version
  • The hospital system or workflow involved, if applicable
  • A short description of what happened
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if available

Please do not send more sensitive information than necessary.

Important limits

  • Limbo Health is not an emergency service.
  • Limbo Health does not provide medical advice.
  • We cannot guarantee that a hospital or third party will accept or fulfill a records request.
